# Assignment 5(c) — custom scene

## What was requested

Create an original scene with at least 20,000 particles, several seconds of simulation and a showcase video of at least 10 seconds.

## What was implemented

24,000 initial particles, 3 seconds, 151 frames, 186 removed particles. The 10.07-second video plays the output at 15 FPS.
